3 · Who is in the room
The crew lead

Gets the text. Taps the link, or just replies to the text. AQUÍ, FOTOS, LISTO, FALTA MATERIAL, a line to the supervisor. Never a login. Whoever holds the phone is the crew; he types his name once.

Luis · Obed · Robert · Mike

Pushed on every tap. Read and answer in the crew's lane, from the app or the site. Their answer reaches the crew by text and on the link. Luis signs off on the LISTO photos.

Sam · Laura · Jonathan · Jess · Kevin

See the whole room on the customer's file. Pick @crew in the note box and the note goes to the crew, texted, without anyone looking up a number. Jonathan's material run comes off the FALTA MATERIAL ask.

The customer

Not in this room, ever. Their texts live on their own line on the same file; the crew never sees them and they never see the crew's lane. When the switch for the chain's texts is on, they hear "the crew arrived" from the main line.
