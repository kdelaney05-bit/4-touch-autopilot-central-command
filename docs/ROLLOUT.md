# The rollout — the morning of 16 Sep 2026, and the week after

Kevin, 3 AM: "need full go-to-market for this entire ecosystem… how we roll out
in the morning. we need to show the problem, acknowledge, and dazzle them with
wow." This is the run of show, the words, the click path, and the week.

## The shape: problem → acknowledge → wow (12 minutes, one screen)

### 1. The problem (2 minutes, no screen)
Kevin, in his own words, something like:

> "Yesterday somebody texted the office 'is my permit in.' Sam texted the rep.
> The rep called Jess. Jess looked it up and called Sam. Sam texted the
> customer. Four people, forty minutes, and nobody wrote it down. That
> happens twenty times a day. It is not anybody's fault. It is that the job
> lives in five places: a text chain, a Post-it, Contractors Cloud, somebody's
> memory, and a group chat nobody can find."

### 2. Acknowledge (1 minute)
> "You have been carrying that in your heads and your phones for years and it
> worked because you are good. I am not asking you to work harder. I am
> taking the carrying off you. Nothing about how you do your job changes
> today. This is the first step, and the first step is just learning how we
> talk in one place."

### 3. The wow (8 minutes, the screen — the click path below)
Open the site on the big screen, signed in as Kevin. Do these in order. Every
one takes seconds and every one is real.

| # | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open **The Line** | "Everything with a human waiting on the other end. One screen." |
| 2 | In **Say it**, type a customer's last name, pick **Sam**, type "customer wants to move the start to Monday", **Post** | "That reached Sam. Watch her phone." (Sam's phone buzzes if the app is on; Central Command bings if open.) Point at the confirmation: "and it tells me who it reached." |
| 3 | Open Sam's screen on a second laptop, or **View as → Samantha** | "This is Sam's view. There it is in You're up. She presses ✓ Got it." |
| 4 | Open the customer's **file** | "The whole story in one thread. Their texts in green, our notes in blue. Under my note: who it reached, and a check when they open it." |
| 5 | Press **＋ Photo**, take one with the laptop camera or the phone | "Photos live on the customer now. The words, who it's for, the crew, the dollars. The office sees it in the Photos room the same second." |
| 6 | Open **Photos** | "Every picture on every file. Mike runs Oasis off this." |
| 7 | Back on **The Line**, point at **What they are asking → Answer** | "When a customer asks where their permit is, the file already knows and writes the reply. Sam reads it, fixes a word, presses Send. It never sends by itself." |
| 8 | Show **Gio's Quotes to price** card (View as → Gio) | "A rep on a hard fence job taps ASK GIO on his phone. The checklist and the pictures land here. Gio types a price. The rep's phone buzzes. Forty minutes becomes ten." |
| 9 | Play the **film** on the wall while people take a breath: `?demo=1&as=office&tour=1&auto=1` | "It runs itself. Watch it once at your desk." |

Then hand out the two things to try (below) and stop. Do not show every room.
