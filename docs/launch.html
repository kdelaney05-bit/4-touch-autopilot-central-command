If something goes wrong

  • Somebody can't log in: the sign-in page has "Set my password," a fresh link comes to their email. Or tell me and I mint one.
  • No sound when tagged: they have not pressed Turn the bing on, or the tab is closed. The tag is still in their You're up.
  • The app looks the same on a phone: close it fully and reopen; the restart pulls the new version. If the phone is on 1.0.1 to 1.0.3, it needs the older-runtime publish.
  • A stuck popup: reload the page. Escape or a tap anywhere closes it now.
  • Anything else: tell me the customer and the minute. Everything is on the file.
