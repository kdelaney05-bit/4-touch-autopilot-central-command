Nothing to learn. It comes to you.

None of this is something to memorize. It is what is going to happen. Today the office hunts and figures it out; this is the opposite. The machine does the work, and when it needs a person, a card shows up on the right screen that says what it is and what to press. Your job is the one decision only you can make.

What each person keeps

Nobody's job goes to the machine. The retyping does. The part that needs a person gets the whole person.

JonathanThe yes. Is this the right job, is the price what we ordered, does it get paid. One tap, with the order beside it.
ClaudetteThe money. Paying, the exceptions, the statement. Every bill already in QuickBooks with its number, so her time goes to what's wrong, not what's missing.
JessThe conversation. The phone, the customers, the team. Not a single forward.
