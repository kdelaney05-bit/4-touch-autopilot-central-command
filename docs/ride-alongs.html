The Ride-Alongs

A Ride-Along is a short film that runs itself on the real screen. It shows every step, says it out loud, and writes it under the picture. Lookers, listeners and readers all get it, in about two minutes, from one link on their phone. Anything new, important and cool gets one. Text the link. That's the whole training.

How to send one

  • Copy the Play with voice link and text it. It plays on a phone, a laptop, or the wall.
  • Voice is the phone's own reader. If it is quiet, the subtitles carry it; tap ▶ on the card to move at your own pace.
  • Nothing in a Ride-Along is real: the demo book, the demo crew. Nothing is saved.
  • New ones land here. The newest is on top.

The rule

Every change we ship is fast, often, small, and solves one problem. So every one that matters comes with a Ride-Along: two minutes, on the real screen, shown, said and written. If it can't be shown in two minutes, it isn't finished. And the form itself keeps improving: whoever builds the next one is asked to make it easier to find, faster to watch and simpler to send than the last. Rule 31.
