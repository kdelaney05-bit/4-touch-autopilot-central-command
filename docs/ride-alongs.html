The Ride-Alongs

Lookers, listeners and readers all get it, in about two minutes, from one link on their phone. Anything new, important and cool gets one. Text the link. That's the whole training.

How to send one

  • Copy the Play with voice link and text it. It plays on a phone, a laptop, or the wall.
  • A voiced film opens on Sound on. Tap play. Phones refuse to speak until you tap once, so that tap is the whole trick. Then Guy, our narrator, reads every step and the film moves on when he is done. Same voice on every phone.
  • Reading, seeing and hearing at once is the point: "like reading a book in stereo." If someone finds the voice distracting, send them the Subtitles-only link.
  • Nothing in a Ride-Along is real: the demo book, the demo crew. Nothing is saved.
  • New ones land here. The newest is on top. Today's rollout list, one at a time: today.html.
  • The phone app's Ride-Along (PHOTOS card, Send quote to Gio) is next: it has to be filmed in the app, not on this site. Until then, the visuals page shows every phone screen.

The rule

Every change we ship is fast, often, small, and solves one problem. So every one that matters comes with a Ride-Along: two minutes, on the real screen, shown, said and written. If it can't be shown in two minutes, it isn't finished. And the form itself keeps improving: whoever builds the next one is asked to make it easier to find, faster to watch and simpler to send than the last. Rule 31.
