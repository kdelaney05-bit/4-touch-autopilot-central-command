Where the change orders go

Kevin: "where do we put any change orders? I want to make sure that we don't miss anything." Here.

1On the file, next to Estimate: Change order. Only what changed — the extra gate, the longer run, a credit as a negative price. 2The customer taps ACCEPT on the same kind of link they signed the estimate on. 3Signed = a line on the invoice, with its own amount and date. Unsigned = the invoice waits, red: "1 change order unsigned · Add one 4' gate $850." 4A change order signed on paper still counts: the office puts the signed sheet on the file with the amount typed. No amount typed = red, it says so.
Nothing is missed because the machine does not read anyone's memory. It reads the file: the accepted estimate, every signed change order, the deposit, the sign-off's photos. What is not on the file is not on the invoice, and the card names it.

It stops itselfThe second the money lands. The second the customer writes back — then it is a conversation and the office has it; the reminders wait until the office has answered. And on Stop the reminders, with a reason.
It keeps the hoursTexts 9 AM–7:30 PM only. Call cards and pushes 8 AM–7:30 PM. Nothing overnight, ever (the quiet-hours rule). A step more than three days late is skipped, not fired late.
It knows who said STOPNo texts to a customer who opted out; the email and the call still go. No email on file: the text carries the link. Neither: the card is red before anything goes out.
The phone stays humanThe machine never calls anyone. It puts a card on Laura or Jess that says who, why, and what to ask, with a clock. That is the one touch.
