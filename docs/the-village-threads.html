Best for: you, reading the whole company in one look. Not for: writing. Nobody types into a column; a tap opens the conversation. Same data as A, turned sideways, so it can be a switch on A's screen: "by conversation · by person".

My pick: A, with C as a switch on it, and B's habits.

The board is the one screen that answers everything you said: all the conversations at once, what is being done in each, faces instead of typing @, Sales one tap away, a reply that splits off into its own little thread, and you never leave Central Command. C is the same cards turned by person, one switch at the top, for the owner's read of the room. From B we take the two habits people already have: Enter sends, and the group you talked to last time is saved with the faces as its name.

  • Start one: tap the dashed card, tap faces, type, Enter. The faces are the name. The Sales, Office, Production and Everyone cards are always there.
  • Jump in one: tap the card. Waiting longest is on top, same law as The Line.
  • Split off: Reply under a line. The little thread carries only the people who need it; the main conversation stays clean.
  • About a customer is a small optional chip under the box, not the first thing you see. When it is set, the note lands on their file too.
  • Who sees what: you see every card. An employee sees the cards with their face on them. Nobody can be left out of a conversation about them: tagging a name adds their face.

What it takes: one table for conversations and who is in them, a thread column on the messages, and the new screen in Central Command. A day, plus its note and film. Nothing on the phones changes; the reps' sales thread and the pushes stay exactly as they are.
