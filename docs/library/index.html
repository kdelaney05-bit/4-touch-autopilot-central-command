The Library.

Everything that changed, in about two minutes each. Every piece has three or four pictures of what it is, a few lines on what it does, and a film that shows exactly how it works. Tap a person to see only your pieces.

How to watch. Tap ▶ Play. Sound on. A film opens on the real screen with the words read out loud and written under the picture. Open the real screen takes you to the same spot with pretend customers, so you can try it without touching anything real. New pieces go on top.
