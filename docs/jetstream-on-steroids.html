The rules the picture is built on

What we took from the others

Each of the big tools got one thing right. We are taking that one thing from each and leaving the rest.

What is already true today, and what this adds

Already built in Central Command
  • The customer file thread: texts on any line, emails, team notes with tags, step lines like "Sam settled the permit".
  • Tag = a push and a Tagged list for that person.
  • Four rooms beside the reps' hype thread: Sales, Office, Production, the village. A post can hang on a customer.
  • The supervisor app on TestFlight: Today, Sites, Crews, Pay, live files.
This picture adds · in build order
  • A name and a color on every bubble. One color per person the day they join, everywhere.
  • The manager loop. Tag Obed and Luis is in. Tag Sam and Jess is in. Tag a rep and Gio is in. The tagger stays until it's handled.
  • Listen. Kevin's switch on any thread. On means every message pings his phone.
  • Quick messages. Anyone to anyone, from inside the file, without leaving it.
  • The crew side. A no-login link for subs: work order, photos, "here", "done". Their words land in the file, marked crew.
  • Documents and calls in the timeline. One line each, at the moment they happened.
What it replaces
  • Internal emails about a customer. If it's about a file, it's a note on the file.
  • Calling the office to ask "where is the permit". The thread says "Now: permit, waiting on the county, 4 days".
  • Texting a crew from a personal phone. The crew link is the only line, and it is saved.
  • Group chats that nobody can find later. The rooms are the group chats, and every post can hang on a file.
